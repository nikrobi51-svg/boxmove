Service details

This is useful when wardrobes, beds, tables or shelving do not pass through narrow doors, corridors or elevators. Photos and approximate dimensions help us understand the work before arrival.

Who this service is for

  • disassembling wardrobes, beds, tables or shelving
  • preparing furniture for an apartment move
  • moving large furniture through narrow access points
  • assembling furniture after delivery

How it works

  1. Send photos and describe the furniture.
  2. We clarify dimensions, fasteners, floor and elevator access.
  3. The team disassembles and prepares items for loading.
  4. After delivery, assembly can be completed by agreement.

Price

Price depends on the number of items, complexity of fasteners, working time, floor access, packing and whether assembly is needed after transport.

Service area

We work across Tbilisi districts and arrange routes from Tbilisi to other cities in Georgia. In the city, service is available in Vake, Saburtalo, Didi Dighomi, Gldani, Isani, Samgori and central districts.
