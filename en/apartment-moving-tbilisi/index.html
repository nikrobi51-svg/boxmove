Service details

An apartment move usually needs more preparation than a simple delivery. We clarify the list of items, floors, elevator access, packing, furniture disassembly and number of movers so the move is calm and predictable.

Who this service is for

  • moving from one apartment to another
  • transporting furniture, appliances and boxes
  • partial moves or single large items
  • moves with movers and packing

How it works

  1. Prepare an approximate item list or send photos.
  2. We clarify addresses, floors, elevator and packing.
  3. The team arrives with a van and movers.
  4. Items are loaded, transported and unloaded at the new address.

Price

Apartment moving price depends on volume, movers, floors, elevator access, packing, furniture disassembly and distance between addresses.

Service area

We work across Tbilisi districts and arrange routes from Tbilisi to other cities in Georgia. In the city, service is available in Vake, Saburtalo, Didi Dighomi, Gldani, Isani, Samgori and central districts.

Advantages

  • team matched to item volume
  • loading and unloading
  • packing on request
  • furniture disassembly and assembly
