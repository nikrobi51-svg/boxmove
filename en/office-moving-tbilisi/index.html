Service details

Office moving often includes desks, chairs, shelves, documents, monitors, printers and packed equipment. We agree timing, item list and access details in advance to reduce downtime.

Who this service is for

  • moving a small or medium office
  • transporting desks, chairs and shelves
  • moving boxes with documents
  • relocating equipment and work supplies

How it works

  1. Send the item list, photos and addresses.
  2. We clarify floors, elevator, parking and preferred time.
  3. The team arrives with the van and movers.
  4. Items are loaded, transported and placed at the new location.

Price

Office moving is quoted individually based on volume, floors, number of movers, packing, route and agreed working time.

Service area

We work across Tbilisi districts and arrange routes from Tbilisi to other cities in Georgia. In the city, service is available in Vake, Saburtalo, Didi Dighomi, Gldani, Isani, Samgori and central districts.

Advantages

  • careful handling of equipment
